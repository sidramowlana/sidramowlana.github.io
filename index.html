About

I started doing this because I kept finding great little businesses that were hard to find online.

There's a bubble tea place near me with a queue out the door and nothing but an Instagram page. A clinic down the road still takes every booking over the phone. At work I build large systems for companies, and it felt like a waste not to use that for the places I actually go to.

It's just me. You talk to me and I write the code. If you don't need something, I'll tell you, and you'll know the price before we start.

Working together

Here's roughly how it goes.

Every project is a bit different, but this is what usually happens.

  1. 01

    We have a chat

    You fill in the form or we jump on a call. I'll ask about your business, who your customers are and what you want to spend.

  2. 02

    I show you a design

    You'll see what the site will look like before I write any code. Change whatever you don't like.

  3. 03

    I build it

    I'll send you a link every so often so you can click around and see how it's going.

  4. 04

    It goes live

    I put it on your domain and show you how to change text and photos yourself.

  5. 05

    I stick around

    If something breaks or you want to add something later, just message me.

Questions
How long does a project take?

A Starter site takes 1–2 weeks. Bigger sites and apps take 3–12 weeks depending on features. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you work with clients outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. I work remotely with clients anywhere and keep you updated over email, WhatsApp or video calls in your time zone.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% at launch. Larger projects are paid in milestones as each stage is delivered.

I don't have a design or logo yet.

That's fine. I'll design the look and flow with you before building, and can suggest colours and fonts that suit your business.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Once it's paid in full, the code, design and accounts are yours.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a support period. After that, care plans cover updates, backups, fixes and small changes.
